# Station Points

A station-based grocery finder for residents of Japan. Select a train station and point memberships to compare nearby food retailers, earning and redemption conditions, and advertised discounts by date. English and Japanese store names; no account needed. Use cited real branch records and dated chain rules. Unknown point support and missing sale data remain unknown. Do not infer loyalty-card earning from a payment logo. Dates are Japan dates. First rollout uses researched Tokyo stations; coverage is explicit.

Open-source multilingual MiniLM enables English and Japanese semantic search over source-reviewed Japanese notice paraphrases. A reproducible ONNX pipeline builds the index and evaluates retrieval. It does not extract prices or determine eligibility. Dates and walk-time sorting are deterministic. Model outputs are not ground truth. Keep per-query evaluation results and source provenance. No made-up prices, stock, live refresh or universal coverage claims. No bank login, purchased data or credentials in the client.

The selector includes 74 stations across the requested routes and the three original pilot stations. Every station has at least five sourced candidates; compare up to ten distinct retailers within 2.5 km or official station access, with other branches expandable. The number of different retailers varies; missing retailers must not be concealed by repeated branches. Label straight-line estimates separately from published walks. Keep branch facts, date-bounded flyers and reviewed point rules separate, with source check times and expiry handling. Program guides do not establish local support. The semantic index contains 20 reviewed notices; its 40-query evaluation is an authored smoke fixture.
